# Rebocap Motion Capture Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Shenzhen Ruoying Intelligent Capture Technology Co., Ltd. (深圳若影智捕科技有限公司, “Rebocap”, “we” or “us”) handles information when you use the Rebocap Motion Capture app for Android or Meta Horizon OS (the “App”).

If you have questions or want to exercise your privacy rights, contact **official@rebocap.com**.

## Information processed by the App

**Motion capture and device information.** When you connect compatible Rebocap equipment, the App processes information from the receiver and trackers, such as device status, motion, orientation and calibration data. It also processes settings you provide, such as skeleton and body configuration. Depending on how they are used, motion and body-related information may relate to an identifiable person.

**Authorization information.** The App obtains an Android device identifier and uses it in the authorization process. To verify authorization, the App communicates with our authorization service. The service receives the request time, a signature fingerprint and IP address. We use this information to validate authorization and operate the App.

**Settings, recordings and logs.** The App stores settings on your device. If you choose to record motion, the App can save the recording on your device for playback or use with compatible tools. The App may also generate or display operational information and logs to help you use and troubleshoot it.

**Support communications.** If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as your message, screenshots, logs or recordings. Please avoid sending information that is not needed to answer your request.

## How information is used and shared

We use the information described above to authorize the App, connect and manage compatible equipment, display and calibrate tracking, support motion recording and playback, troubleshoot problems, and respond to your requests.

When you enable motion output or connect another application or device, the App may send motion data and related information to the destination you configure, including over your local network. The receiving application or device handles that information according to its own settings and privacy practices. Please use these features only with destinations you trust and protect your local network.

The App does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions or in-app user-to-user chat. Meta and other platforms through which you obtain or use the App may process information under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect legitimate rights and safety.

## Storage and retention

Authorization request information is processed by the authorization service for verification and is not retained in cloud storage after processing. A successful authorization session may be stored on your phone or headset for approximately 15 days.

App settings, recordings and other local files remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App, depending on the device and its storage settings. Files you have exported or shared, and device or cloud backups managed by you or another provider, may remain after removal. We retain support correspondence for the time needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

## Your choices and rights

You can stop collecting new motion data by disconnecting the equipment or stopping the relevant feature. You can manage recordings using the App or your device's file controls, and clear the App's local data or uninstall it using your device settings. Clearing data or uninstalling may remove settings and locally stored recordings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us at **official@rebocap.com** to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law requires or permits it.

## Security and international use

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Information may be processed in China or another location where the App or its services operate. If information is transferred across borders, we will handle it in accordance with applicable law.

## Children

The Meta Horizon Store version of the App is intended for users aged 13 and older, or the applicable higher minimum age in their region. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can review and address the matter.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or our practices change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the date above. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice.
